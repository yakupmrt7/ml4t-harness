- Second, fully independent implementation on daily 04:00 closes (no harness): price-only SR 1.11 vs the script's 0.99; reversal -0.75 vs -0.65. Same signs, same order of magnitude; the gap is 5m-summed vs daily-compounded arithmetic. No sign or alignment error.

## Blocking issues
These are claim-level fixes in RESULTS.md. None changes the verdict.

1. **"Its edge per trade (21 bps) comfortably exceeds cost (8.8)" overclaims.** The breakeven is a mean, and it inherits the same t. Breakeven = sum(gross)/turnover = 2.73/1300 units -> 21.0 bps with s.e. = std*sqrt(n)/turnover = 0.0899*sqrt(610)/1300 = **17.1 bps**. One s.e. down is 3.9 bps, below the 8.8 cost. Say "21 +/- 17 bps" and drop "comfortably"; the same applies to "Costs stopped being the binding constraint" (true for the point estimate only).
2. **The beta warning is asserted, not measured, and for momentum it is wrong.** I measured it: regressing the momentum book's daily P&L on the equal-weight universe return over the same window gives **beta = -0.22** (on a 3-long/3-short, gross-6 book), and beta-hedging *raises* the price-only Sharpe from 1.11 to 1.18. The long leg has beta +2.9 and the short leg -3.1; they cancel. The observed long-leg-wins/short-leg-loses pattern is just the market drift passing through both legs, which is what any neutral book shows in an up market -- it is not evidence of residual beta. Replace the paragraph with the measured beta (one line of code, data already loaded), or delete the claim.
3. **Day count.** RESULTS says "608 days" and "t = SR*sqrt(608/365)"; the code uses 609 decisions and **610** daily buckets (`selection.json: "days": 610`, and the trial rows log n = 610). The 610th is a degenerate empty bucket at 2022-07-31 (the grid's first bar, held = 0). I checked: dropping it moves SR 0.950 -> 0.951 and leaves t at 1.229, so the numbers are fine, but the report must state the count the code actually used, and the 2022 row is 154 buckets = 153 real days + the empty one.

## Non-blocking issues
- **Causality: clean, and in any case the kill is robust to it.** Checked by reading the code: 04:00 close decisions with `delay=1` (house convention: fill at the decision close, documented in `backtest.py`); factors use only trailing data (`close.reindex(times - DAY)`, `prem.rolling(12).mean()` at t, `fund.rolling(9).sum()` joined with strict `asof_join` so the 00:00 settlement is the newest one used at 04:00); daily bucketing `(index - 4h05m).floor('1D')` puts exactly the bars 04:05..next-04:00 into decision day d, verified by hand; weights are ffilled onto the 5m grid at their own 04:00 stamp and then shifted one bar by the backtester. No `shift(-k)`, no `center=True`, no scaler or selection fitted on anything. The script's self-check (recompute at 5 random dates from data truncated at that date) would catch a `shift(-k)` but not a wrong index convention (e.g. premium index stamped by open time); `check_close_alignment` exists in the harness and was not used on the premium series. Low priority because every direction of leakage here inflates the result, and the result still fails.
- **Effective n:** I checked what rule 7 asks for. Daily P&L autocorrelation is lag-1 -0.08, lags 2-5 within +/-0.03; Newey-West(10) t = 1.26 vs iid 1.23. So n_eff ~= n here and the quoted t is honest. Worth stating in RESULTS rather than leaving implicit, since positions persist ~6 days.
- **Funding timing** in `funding_pnl` charges the settlement to the position held over the bar *after* the stamp (searchsorted left on a stamp a few ms past the mark), i.e. one 5m bar late. It matches `run_backtest` exactly (new test does this) and settlements at 00/08/16 are never adjacent to the 04:00 decision, so it cannot matter here. Fine as is.
- **Cost is conservative, which never drove a kill.** taker_all_in (5 bps fee + measured half-spread, tick-floored) = 5.0-7.0 per asset, plus an impact add-on taken as the *mean* effective half-spread of the $10-50k bucket minus quoted at order times. For a $10k order that bucket mean is dominated by larger orders, so 8.8-9.0 bps is if anything too high; UNFI's +14.08 add-on rests on a thin two-day sample. It does not matter: all four factors fail on t, and the one that passes the cost leg passes it at 2.4x. Charging on `|dw|` only (a position that stays in its leg does not trade) is correct.
- **No null test was run.** A `portfolio_shift_null` on the daily weight matrix (keeps turnover and cross-asset structure, destroys timing) is ~seconds here and would put momentum's 0.95 in a distribution. Not required for a kill, but cheap context for the next round.
- **`gross_long_share`** (13.4 for carry, -0.78 for reversal) divides by a near-zero denominator and is meaningless; either drop the column or report leg P&L in bps.
- **Design critique, not a fault:** t >= 2 on 608 days demands an annualised Sharpe >= 1.55 from a single-factor, 3-long/3-short book in a 15-asset universe. A genuinely good crypto factor (true SR ~0.8) has an expected t of 1.0 here. The kill was pre-registered and must stand, but it could not have distinguished "no edge" from "good edge", and the experiment's real output is a power measurement. Design the next one for power, not for another threshold.

## What the evidence supports
- Carry (3d cumulative funding), 1-day basis change, 1-day reversal and 28d/skip-1 momentum, sorted 3v3 daily on these 15 perps over 2022-08 -> 2024-03, do not produce a gross Sharpe distinguishable from zero. Best t 1.61 (basis change), and the 1 s.e. on any of these Sharpes is 0.78.
- 1-day cross-sectional reversal is negative (SR -0.61, t -0.79). This is a genuine piece of information: the short-horizon reversal that p01-p04 chased does not exist at the 1-day cross-sectional horizon, at least not with this sign.
- Carry's gross is 8.2x funding: the price leg loses (-0.65) roughly as fast as the funding is collected. That is the textbook result -- funding is compensation for taking the crowded side, not a free coupon -- and it is worth remembering before anyone proposes a funding-harvest strategy again.
- The horizon change did what p05's review predicted: turnover fell to 778 units/yr and the edge per trade rose to ~21 bps against ~9 bps of measured cost. The cost wall is no longer the binding constraint at a 1-day horizon. Sample size is.
- The four factors are close to orthogonal (mean cross-sectional rank correlation -0.07..+0.08), so no combination of them would have reached t = 2 either.
- Not supported: anything about momentum being a real effect. Its 2024-Q1 gross SR is -2.89 against +2.07 in 2023, and its full-sample t is 1.23.

## Proposed next experiment (p07): does the momentum sort replicate on untouched history?
- **Hypothesis (falsifiable):** the 28d/skip-1 cross-sectional momentum sort, book construction frozen exactly as in p06, has a positive gross Sharpe on 2020-10-01 -> 2022-07-31, a window no experiment in this repo has ever read. Falsified if that window's gross Sharpe is <= 0.
- **Exact change vs p06:** one variable -- the sample. Same factor, same sign, same 04:00 decision, same 3-long/3-short, same delay=1, same daily bucketing, same universe (whichever of the 15 are listed; require >= 8 live, which starts around 2020-10). Drop the other three factors. New data: 5m klines + funding for the 15 assets, 2020-01 -> 2022-07 (`scripts/download_binance.py`); 28 days of warm-up before the first decision.
- **Why this over the alternatives:** it is the only cheap test that separates "the 2022-24 t of 1.23 is a real SR ~1 factor" from "it is noise", and 2020-10 -> 2022-07 is a different regime (the 2021 bull, the May-21 and Nov-21 crashes, funding regularly at 100%+ annualised), so a replication there is worth more than the same number of extra days appended to a quiet period.
- **Cost treatment (pre-register now, so no cost work is wasted):** use a flat **12 bps per unit traded** for the net/economic leg. That is above every 2022-24 measured value (8.8-9.0) and allows for 2020-21 spreads being wider; the p05 table does not cover pre-2022-08 and must NOT be extrapolated backwards. Rebuild the real cost table for the new months only if the statistical gate below passes.
- **Pre-OOS selection plan:** zero free parameters. Nothing is chosen on the new window; the factor and the book were fixed in p06's PLAN. Report on the new window alone, on the p06 window alone, and pooled: gross SR with s.e. 1/sqrt(years), t, NW(10) t, breakeven +/- s.e., turnover, beta to the equal-weight universe and the beta-hedged SR, per-year SR, and a `portfolio_shift_null` p-value. One row in `selection_trials.json`. `trials.json` untouched.
- **Kill criterion, fixed in advance:** abandon daily cross-sectional momentum (and with it the single-factor crypto-xsec line) if the 2020-10 -> 2022-07 gross Sharpe is <= 0, or if its breakeven is < 12 bps. Proceed to the OOS gate only if **both** windows have gross SR >= +0.5 **and** the pooled t >= 2.0 **and** the pooled breakeven minus one s.e. >= 12 bps. Do not tune anything in between; if the pooled t lands in 1.5-2.0 with both windows positive, that is an honest "underpowered, not dead" and the follow-up is more breadth (see below), never another threshold.
- **If it passes and you want more power before spending the OOS trial:** the one pre-registered upgrade allowed is breadth -- rank-demeaned weights across all live assets instead of 3v3, and a wider universe -- chosen on the 2020-2022 window only and re-confirmed on 2022-08 -> 2024-03 before the gate. That costs a cost-table rebuild for any new asset, so do it only after the replication passes.
- **Expected cost:** 1 selection trial, 0 OOS trials. ~470 kline zips + funding (about the same download as p04), and a run of the same order as p06's 272 s.
- **Line-abandonment note:** p01-p05 (5m bar-close mean reversion) is already dead and was correctly abandoned. p06 is failure #1 for the daily cross-sectional line, so it is too early to abandon it. If p07 fails its kill, the count on daily crypto factor sorts is 2; one more failure (e.g. the breadth variant) and the pivot should be away from price/funding-derived factors on Binance perps entirely -- towards the collector data (Polymarket CLOB vs Binance, Chainlink), which is the only genuinely non-public-archive edge in this repo.
