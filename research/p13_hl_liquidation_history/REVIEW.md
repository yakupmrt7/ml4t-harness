# Review: p13_hl_liquidation_history (stage 2 event study, dev only)
**Decision:** ACCEPT WITH FIXES (the kill stands; the fixes are about wording and record-keeping, not the verdict)
**One line:** On HL dev (2025-07 → 2026-06, 39,079 windows, 21,109 clusters, 221 effective days), a taker fade entered 2 s after a liquidation gets median reversion of +0.4 / +0.7 / +2.1 bps at 60 / 120 / 300 s, against a 9.8 bps round trip. The mean is about 0 (−0.5 / −0.6 / +0.2 bps), so there is no gross edge even before costs. Kill 2 holds under every check I ran.

## What I verified myself
- **Pre-registration.** The step 2 addendum is in commit b0eee85 (10:16). `book_windows.py` and `windows.py` have mtimes of 10:18 and 10:17, and `wm_box.parquet` 13:08. PLAN.md has not been changed since. The kill is computed as registered (`kill2()`): median rev ≥ median cost10k_rt at 60/120/300 s, for "all" and for "top", on the ≤ 2 s cadence set. No trial is logged, no harness files were changed, and nothing from the holdout was read (`windows.py` cuts at t0 + 302 s < 2026-06-11 02:00).
- **Signs.** s = sign(buy − sell liquidated notional), with 'A' = the liquidated user sold, so s = −1 when longs are liquidated and d = +1. As a sanity check, pre_move = s·(mid(t0)/mid(t0−60 s) − 1) is +9 to +16 bps in every decile. The price moved in the push direction before the liquidations, which is how liquidations must look. No sign error.
- **Reversion arithmetic.** I recomputed rev5/60/120 and pre_move from the independent 1 s mid grids in `mids1s.npz`. The max abs difference from `window_metrics.parquet` is 0.0 on all 43,592 windows.
- **Cost.** cost10k_rt = buy10k + sell10k (each is VWAP vs mid, so each already contains the half-spread) + 9 bps. The median is 9.78, of which 0.78 is spread + impact. That matches PLAN (full spread + 2 × 4.5 bps taker + impact). The exit-time-book variant has median 9.82/9.82/9.81, so it makes no difference.
- **Direction look-ahead (focus 1).** s uses the whole cluster-coin window, including liquidations after t0 + 2 s. I recomputed s from events with t0 ≤ window t0 + 2 s only. It agrees with the original s in 99.3% of windows (271 flips). The causal-s medians are +0.44 / +0.77 / +2.10 bps, against the reported +0.40 / +0.72 / +2.05. The effect is negligible.
- **Size look-ahead (focus 1, the important one).** In the pre-registered "top decile" (≥ $758k window notional), the median share of the notional that is known at entry is **1%**. The set is almost entirely defined by what happened afterwards. With deciles of **notional known at t0 + 2 s**, the causal top decile (≥ $72k known) gives rev **+1.4 / +1.6 / +2.3 bps** against a cost of 9.5. The causal deciles are flat, with rev300 between +0.9 and +2.6 in all ten. The continuation of −8.8 / −12.9 / −13.7 bps is therefore **entirely** a selection-on-outcome artefact. The kill still holds for large events defined causally.
- **n < 10 snapshot guard / per-coin loading (focus 2).** The guard counts snapshots and never looks at prices, and per-coin loading only reorders the work, so both are outcome-neutral. Of the 3,678 windows without books, 3,565 fall in the two S3 gaps and **113 are scattered elsewhere**. RESULTS says all 3,678 are in the two gaps. That is a minor misstatement. The backstop/ADL exclusion was made in step 1 before any outcome was read. The reasoning holds: backstop liquidations transfer the position to the vault without trading in the book, so they are not forced flow. It is documented in STEP1.md and in the events.py docstring.
- **Stability.** Monthly median rev300 is between 0.0 and +4.1 bps in every month and never approaches the cost (9.5–10.3). By side, s = −1 gives +2.1 and s = +1 gives +2.0. It is not one regime and not beta.
- **Maker-exit variant** (pre-registered in PLAN "Definitions" as "reported alongside", but missing from RESULTS). My approximation is (entry-side taker cost) + 4.5 + 1.5 ≈ 6.4 bps median, still above 2.05. Even with the 9 bps of fees removed entirely, the round trip is 0.78 bps against a gross **mean** of +0.19 at 300 s.

## Non-blocking issues
- **The 5 s downsample check is mis-specified as a tradeability test.** It measures the P&L as if you traded at the stale snapshot mid, but a live order executes at the live price. The liquidation trigger itself comes from the trade/fill stream, which is not limited by the 5 s book cadence. "Whatever reversion exists at 0.5 s is not reachable at today's cadence" does not follow from it. Replace it with: "with 5 s book snapshots the effect cannot be measured, and tradeability depends on execution latency, which was not measured." The kill does not depend on this.
- **The depletion/refill claims have no placebo.** Decile 0 (liquidations of $0–178, which cannot move a BTC book) shows depth falling to 42% of baseline, and 7% of windows fall below 1%. The min-over-60 s of a flickering 20-level book is mostly noise. Also, "within 50 bps" is capped by the 20 levels, so it is really top-20-level depth. Either add random non-event windows as a baseline or soften "books deplete and refill in ~30 s" to "descriptive, no baseline".
- Two variants that PLAN registered as "reported alongside" or "reported, not used" are missing from RESULTS: maker-exit and exit-time-book cost. Add one line for them.
- **Distribution.** ROADMAP §4 asks for the distribution, not just the median. rev300 quantiles p5/p25/p50/p75/p95 are −64 / −15 / +2.1 / +18.6 / +58 bps. Put this in RESULTS: the median is positive but the mean is 0. That is the payoff shape of selling insurance (market jargon: short gamma, i.e. many small gains and a few large losses). Fading forced flow on HL earns small amounts often and gives it back in the cascades that keep going.
- PLAN says the window must satisfy t0 + 300 s < dev end, while the code uses t0 + 302 s. This is immaterial, but note it.
- The teardown section promised in RESULTS is missing. The IAM role `p13-hl-archive-read` is still attached to tokyo-vm, and `~/p13` is still on the box. Detach or delete them and append the section.
- `trials_hl.json` does not exist in the repo root. That is fine because no trial was run, but PLAN refers to it as if it existed.

## What the evidence supports
- Kill 2 fires cleanly and is not an artefact of sign, staleness (entry snapshot gap median 0.8 s, max 2 s), cost arithmetic, direction look-ahead or size look-ahead. The margin is large: median net is −8 to −10 bps and the gross mean is ≈ 0. For the kill to flip, you would need a cost about 5× lower or a reversion about 5× larger.
- The market reading: HL liquidations fire after the move (+9 to +16 bps in the preceding 60 s, because liquidations trigger off a mark that lags). Other participants absorb the forced flow at a spread of ~3 bps within seconds, and what follows is roughly a coin flip with fat tails.
- **What was actually tested:** taker in and out, a $10k clip, entry 2 s after the first liquidation of the coin-window, fixed exits at 60/120/300 s, and six coins.
- **Not tested:**
  - sub-2 s entry;
  - passive bids resting ahead of the cascade (ROADMAP §5's "passive" rule, i.e. literally "being the liquidity");
  - entry at cascade exhaustion or after a notional threshold is crossed;
  - the stage-0 visibility of liquidation levels in advance.

  "The line closes" is justified because PLAN pre-registered exactly this kill as closing the line. The "Dead:" sentence should list the tested scope instead of implying all forms of "being the liquidity". My prior on the untested variants is low: the gross mean is ≈ 0 from t0 + 2 s, and a resting bid gets filled precisely in the cascades that continue (adverse selection).
